Julius Mex

Research Fellow

Teleconnections and Regional Climate Change Causal Data Science

Research Interests

My research aims to understand the large scale drivers of regional climate change and the interaction of the forced response with internal variability. In my work I make use of the physical climate storyline approach and extend it to include internal variability to understand, constrain and explain uncertainty in climate projections.
