Frieder Loer

PhD Researcher

Detection and Attribution Extreme Weather and Climate Events Machine Learning

Bio

I studied Physics and Earth System Data Science and Remote Sensing at Leipzig University. Towards the end of my studies, I developed a strong interest in combining data science methods with a research domain of my interest and found a great combination of both in Climate Attribution.

Research Interests

My research interest lies in the application of statistical and machine learning methods for improving the understanding and attribution of air temperature trends and extremes. Ultimately, my research aims to provide attribution statements that support further research on the influence of air temperature extremes on private household economics within the Eco-N graduate school.
